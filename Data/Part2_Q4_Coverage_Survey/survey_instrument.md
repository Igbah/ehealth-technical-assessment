# Post-Campaign Coverage Survey: Household Instrument (extract)

All eligible children aged 9 to 59 completed months resident in the selected household
are enumerated. One questionnaire is completed per household.

## Section 1: Identification
1.1 Cluster identifier
1.2 Household identifier
1.3 Interviewer identifier
1.4 Date of visit
1.5 Result of visit: Completed / Refused / No eligible respondent after three visits / Vacant dwelling

## Section 2: Household roster
2.1 Number of children aged 9 to 59 completed months resident in the household
2.2 Household wealth quintile, assigned from the asset module

## Section 3: Child vaccination status (repeated per eligible child)
3.1 Child identifier
3.2 Age in completed months
3.3 Sex
3.4 Was a vaccination card, card copy, or electronic record seen by the interviewer? Yes / No
3.5 If a card was seen: is the campaign dose recorded on the card? Yes / No
3.6 If no card was seen: does the caregiver report that the child received the campaign dose? Yes / No

## Interviewer instructions
Question 3.5 is asked only where 3.4 is Yes. Question 3.6 is asked only where 3.4 is No.
Where a card is seen, the card takes precedence and caregiver recall is not recorded.
Do not substitute a household that is absent. Record the result of visit and move to the
next selected household. Cluster substitution requires written supervisor authorisation.
